DISPATCHER / باربەڕێکەر
A horizontal row replaces the driver dropdown. The first three drivers are shown
initially. + Driver opens a checklist of existing drivers; choose shortcuts and Save.
Actual new global drivers are created using the existing authorized driver tools.
One shortcut is active at a time, highlighted violet. Tap another to switch rapidly.
Tap the same driver again or Auto to clear selection. Buttons scroll horizontally.
Shortcuts and selection are remembered locally on this browser/device. Shared load
records and transfers still synchronize to the server. Old loading sessions do not
override Auto mode.

LOAD RULES
- Selected driver: load for that driver, adding membership if absent.
- Existing membership under other drivers: Kurdish confirmation names those drivers
  and the selected destination. Confirm transfers membership and loads in ONE queued
  action; Cancel changes nothing. Unrelated markets stay in their lists.
- Auto and an existing owner: use that driver without a driver chooser. If several
  lists contain it, use the first owner in saved driver order and keep other owners.
  Explicit selection and confirmation can consolidate it into the selected list.
- Auto and no owner: choose a driver or Without driver for this market only. The
  next unowned market asks again. Cancel changes nothing.
- Without driver stores blank driver_id/name plus an unassigned marker. It never
  creates a fake driver or adds membership to a driver's list.
- Successful loading clears search and shows Loaded for <name> or Loaded without
  a driver. Attribution is saved with the server loading record and activity.
- Undo restores captured zones as before, but does not move membership back to the
  previous driver. Explicit selection remains until changed or cleared.
- Existing confirmation for markets not placed in any zone remains.

PERMISSIONS
Dispatcher role + load permission are required. Superadmin can control Dispatcher
quick buttons, Transfer while loading and Load without driver independently.
Disabled transfer denies the actual operation. Other roles retain their loading flow.
