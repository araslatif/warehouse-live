Warehouse Placement Supabase Live V3 — Zone Market Lists

Changes:
- Removed the warehouse cell/grid workflow from the UI.
- Zones now contain market lists directly.
- Clicking a zone focuses the market-number/name input.
- Add markets by number or name, with catalog suggestions.
- Each market row can be deleted directly from its zone.
- Find Market marks placed markets and clicking a placed result jumps to its zone and highlights it.
- Placed Markets list is more compact so more records fit on screen.
- Existing old cell assignments are automatically converted in the browser into unique market entries per zone.
- Supabase URL/key and Realtime connection remain unchanged.
- No new SQL migration is required for this version because warehouse_state stores JSON.

Deployment:
Replace/update the repository files and commit to main. Cloudflare will redeploy automatically.
