Warehouse Placement Supabase Live V24

Changes:
- Profile photos can be added from Camera or Gallery.
- Arabic-Indic and Kurdish/Persian digits are automatically normalized to English 0-9 in app inputs, including market codes and access codes.
- Active-users/device button beside Live now uses a yellow background and opens the users/devices list.
- General role sees only currently active users/devices in that list.
- Market Assigner and Super Admin can also see known assigner users while offline, with their profile photo and tracked market-assignment details.
- Existing Supabase data model remains compatible; no SQL migration required.
