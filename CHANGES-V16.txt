V16 - Shared Recycle Bin + Connected Device Details

- Added Delete control to every assigned market in the Placed Markets list for both General and Market Assigner roles.
- Delete always asks for confirmation.
- Deleting a placed market removes it from all currently assigned rows and stores the market + previous row locations in a shared Recycle Bin inside warehouse_state.
- Both General and Market Assigner roles can restore a deleted market to its previous row(s).
- Only Market Assigner can permanently remove records from the Recycle Bin.
- Recycle Bin shows the market number/name, its previous rows, and deletion time.
- Added a Recycle Bin counter/button on the Placed Markets page.
- Made the connected-device count clickable.
- Connected Devices modal shows live Supabase Presence entries with platform/browser, a stable per-browser device ID, current role, and online status.
- No SQL/database schema migration required; recycleBin is stored inside the existing JSON warehouse_state.
